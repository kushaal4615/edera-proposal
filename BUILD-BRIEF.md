# Edera proposal site: brief for section authors

Project: `~/Deropo/edera-proposal` (Vite + React 18 + TS + Tailwind v4 + Framer Motion + lucide-react + @icons-pack/react-simple-icons; fonts self-hosted via @fontsource-variable). Dev server running at **http://localhost:5292** (do not start or stop it; `node_modules` is in place, do not run installs).
Client: **Dave**, building **Edera** (brand written “e-dera.” with a brass dot): an event marketplace and management platform connecting clients (event hosts), event planners, vendors (DJs, bakers, caterers) and venue owners, UK and Nigeria first. Four roles: client, planner, vendor/venue (mobile app and web), super admin (web). Free at launch, commission later; local languages; a hybrid start to the data. Issued price: **$7,000 over 3 months in 8 milestones** (10/10/15/15/15/15/10/10). Support per house rule: **12 months of free bug fixes included, then completely optional** ($500/month for up to 15 hours, $30/hour beyond, opt-in, no lock-in).

## DIRECTION CONTRACT (obey it)
- **Audience read.** Dave reads this alone, on his phone as much as his laptop, after a 33-minute call where he said he wants the best result at the most minimal budget and has asked other developers too. He fears a vague scope, a price that creeps, and a team that cannot show it understood an owambe. He is impressed by a page that already knows his industry, a fixed price with nothing due between items, and ownership spelled out.
- **World: the Order of Programme.** The printed programme booklet handed to every guest at a celebration, British or Nigerian. (1) Ground is cream card stock with grain; the ONE card family is the **card** (`.card`: card stock, a double brass rule inset 0.5rem from the edge, a soft evergreen-tinted shadow; `.card-plain` has no frame, for photo or device cells). (2) Every section opens as an **item on the programme** (`Item`: the item number, the time it is printed at, the item’s name, an optional ruby `Seal`), so the page reads as one evening from doors at 14:00 to the RSVP at 23:00. (3) Numbers are **foil-stamped**: display numerals and money wear `.foil` (brass gradient text, display numerals and amounts ONLY, never body text); times are `Time` stamps; counts in `.struck` mono; the vendor calendar prints as the `Avail` strip (free evergreen, held brass, booked ink).
- **Palette.** Ground `--ground` card cream (never text). Ink `--ink` Edera evergreen `#10332D` (never a fill behind body text; the dark band uses `--ink-band` with `--on-band` text). Primary `--primary` evergreen `#1C6B5A` (rules, active states, the avail strip’s free cells; never body text on card: use `--primary-ink` for evergreen-family text). Money/CTA `--cta` / `--money` brass foil (ONLY the one CTA, the nav RSVP, and every amount; never decoration, never headings; brass text on card uses `--money` `#8A5E14` for contrast). Verification `--verified` ruby `#A5303E` (ONLY the seals and INCLUDED/CONFIRMED stamps; never links, never alerts). Product screens inside devices keep their own `--scr-*` tokens (the prototype’s Evergreen & Brass look) and are never theme-inverted. Dark theme (`[data-theme=dark]`) is the programme read by candlelight: night evergreen ground, cream ink, brighter foil.
- **Type.** Display **Bodoni Moda** (`h1–h3`, `.display`; `.display-italic` for the wordmark; `<em>` inside a heading turns brass italic, one word at most), body **Instrument Sans**, mono **Azeret Mono** (`.mono`, `.struck`, `.kicker`). Hero `--fs-hero`: 42px at 320 / 80px at 768 / 128px at 1440; `--fs-h2` 40 / 56 / 80px; `--fs-fact` for the three cover numbers; body 17px, small 14px, micro 12px.
- **Motion language.** Card stock turning and foil catching the light: reveals use `SETTLE` (`--ease-turn`, 0.6–0.9s); seals, stamps and times land on stiff springs (stiffness 520–600, damping 22–30); foil highlight sweeps on hover. Springs for anything touched (300–500 / 25–35). Animate transform/opacity only. Every animation gated by `useReducedMotion()`; with reduced motion everything renders fully visible and static. Exits faster than entries. Press feedback `whileTap={{ scale: 0.97 }}`.
- **3D plan: Tier A, CSS 3D.** No WebGL: the product is software on devices. `ProductMock` (phone for client, planner and vendor screens; laptop for the super admin; pointer tilt, scroll flattens) breaks the hero grid; `Tilt3D` on cards; the architecture is an exploded isometric stack of five plates; “After the party” is a layered parallax scene.
- **Imagery plan.** Event photographs bundled in `src/assets/img` (`IMG.toast`, `ballroom`, `dj`, `buffet`, `cake`, `drapes`, `qr`, `lagos`, `london`, `confettiKiss`, `balloons`, `tipi`, `tableGreenery`, `glasshouse`, `birthday`) and three portraits (`adaeze` client, `tunde` planner, `bola` vendor). Every photo goes through `Photo` (grayscale, evergreen multiply, card screen, grain) so it reads as a print tipped into the programme; `plain` keeps the colour (use it once at most, for the hero photograph); `eager` only in the first two sections.
- **The surprise: the RSVP.** In the closing band the CTA is the reply card. Pressing it presses a ruby wax `Seal` “ACCEPTED” onto the card, the reply line fills (“Dave · accepts with pleasure”), the eight items of the running order tick off one by one with a brass line (stiff springs, 90ms apart), and the message is copied to the clipboard with a toast. Reduced motion: show the sealed card immediately.

## Hard rules (each broke a previous build)
1. **Content only from `src/data/proposal.ts`** (`P`, `IMG`, `MILESTONES`, `COMMERCIALS`, `TOTAL_COST`, `timelineLabel`, `PROGRAMME`, `PHONE_SCREENS`, `WEB_SCREENS`). Never invent client facts. No hex literals in components (tokens only, `var(--x)`). **No agency or vendor name anywhere** (“we” only). Do not mention Fiverr. Do not write “Kushaal” or “Kunal”.
2. **Your section uses only the archetype assigned to it** below; note it in a comment at the top of the file. Do not reuse another section’s archetype.
3. Grid children get `min-w-0`. Nothing overflows horizontally at 320/375/768/1024/1440.
4. Tailwind v4 reads `text-[var(--x)]` as a COLOUR. Set sizes with `style={{ fontSize: 'var(--fs-h2)' }}` or `text-[length:var(--fs-h2)]`.
5. Sticky/pinned sections: never `overflow: hidden` on the section (it kills `position: sticky`); use `overflow-x: clip`. A sticky panel lives in a column with nothing else in it.
6. Horizontal scrub track: translate by MEASURED overflow (`scrollWidth − clientWidth`, ResizeObserver); first item starts inside the viewport, last ends flush inside it; with reduced motion it scrolls natively (overflow-x auto), never clipped.
7. No button inside a button (copy buttons are siblings of row buttons). No `aria-label` on a bare span/p (use `.sr-only`). `aria-controls` must point at an existing id. Hit targets ≥ 24px (44px for primary). Every external link `target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
8. Text over photos needs a real scrim and ≥ 4.5:1 contrast against the composited background, in both themes.
9. Nothing that carries text may sit under the ProductMock, a seal or a sticky panel at any scroll position at 1440 or 390. Hero facts go in flow BELOW the device on mobile, never absolutely positioned under it.
10. Add `data-intent="verb_noun"` and `data-value="…"` on meaningful controls (hero_screen, flow_step, role_tab, open_layer, open_milestone, copy_figure, open_client_site, open_faq, flip_constraint, rsvp …): the tracker logs them.
11. `opacity < 1` on a child of a `preserve-3d` stack breaks hit testing: dim with a background token instead.
12. Headings are left-set with a kicker, a rule and a number (`Item` does this). Center at most twice on the whole page (Hero and Close only). Max ONE card family (`.card` / `.card-plain`); never card-in-card; never `rounded-xl border shadow-sm`. No two adjacent sections share background, width and alignment.
13. Zero em-dashes (—) and no en-dashes as separators in any visible string you write; use a hyphen, a comma or a middle dot. Data strings are as given.
14. Money and the timeline only through `formatMoney(TOTAL_COST)`, `MILESTONES[i].amount`, `MILESTONES[i].cumulative`, `timelineLabel`. Never type an amount.
